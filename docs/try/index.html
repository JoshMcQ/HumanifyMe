Want it inside Claude Code, Cursor, or your terminal, running locally with your own key?

npx -y humanifyme setup

What happens to your text: your samples and draft are sent once to Anthropic's Claude API to build a voice profile and write the rewrite, after emails, phone numbers and keys are masked. Nothing is stored unless you tick "share" on the feedback form. Your profile stays in this browser. The open-source CLI keeps everything on your machine. Made by Josh McQueary.
